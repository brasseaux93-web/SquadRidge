## 1. Purpose

This bounded pilot evaluates SquadRidge as facilitator-led dialogue infrastructure for [describe program]. It is not a production deployment for unrestricted institutional use.

## 2. Scope

- Maximum rooms: ___
- Maximum concurrent participants: ___
- Pilot period: ___ to ___
- Facilitators authorized: ___

## 3. Data and privacy

- Live dialogue is treated as temporary at the application layer; close-room behavior removes messages from application tables/store.
- Approved commitments may be retained per program policy.
- The software does **not** currently provide end-to-end encryption or certified erasure across backups and logs.
- Partner remains responsible for institutional policy, mandatory reporting, and participant consent language.

## 4. Security posture

- Access must use agreed authentication method (not public demo role picker for live cases).
- Fixture/demo data must never be mixed with live case data.
- Security incidents follow the partner’s notice requirements plus SquadRidge runbooks.

## 5. Limitations

SquadRidge is not a substitute for emergency services, legal counsel, clinical care, or formal investigation processes.

## 6. Evaluation

Success measures focus on process clarity and facilitator utility—not engagement maximization or participant surveillance metrics.

## 7. Exit

At pilot end, data handling follows the written retention plan. Accounts and access are revoked on request.
