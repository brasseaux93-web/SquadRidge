# Participant guide

**Purpose:** Clear expectations for people joining a SquadRidge room.

---

## What to expect

- You appear under a **display role** assigned for the room (for example “Engineer A”), not necessarily your legal name in the transcript UI
- A facilitator guides stages of the conversation
- Live messages are intended to be temporary; when the room closes they are purged from the application
- Only commitments the facilitator approves are meant to remain

## Joining (demo)

1. Open `/enter`
2. Choose **Participant**
3. Open the demo room

## Your responsibilities

- Speak from your experience; follow the room’s working agreements
- Use **Request pause / support** if you need the process to slow down
- Do not assume messages are secret from the facilitator or system operators
- Do not assume the tool prevents screenshots or external notes

## Privacy in plain language

| While room is open | After close (application behavior) |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Members can see dialogue | Live messages removed from app store/tables |
| Facilitator sees process controls | Approved outcomes can remain |

This is **not** a guarantee of anonymity or legal confidentiality. Program rules and institutional policy still apply.
