# Product principles

**Purpose:** Decision criteria for product, design, and engineering choices.

---

1. **Facilitator control** — Process authority stays with trained humans. Software supports structure; it does not decide outcomes.
2. **Dignity** — Participants appear under assigned roles. Language and UI avoid shame, ranking, or pathologizing labels.
3. **Privacy by design** — Collect minimum necessary data. Live dialogue is temporary by default. Durable records require explicit approval paths.
4. **Consent** — Movement of records outside the room requires clear visibility rules and (when implemented) acknowledgement.
5. **Human oversight** — No automated scoring of people, credibility, emotion, or “risk.”
6. **Neutrality** — The platform does not advocate a substantive outcome in a dispute.
7. **Transparency of boundaries** — Demo vs pilot vs production must be obvious. Limitations are stated in product chrome, not only in fine print.
8. **Pilot discipline** — Features ship only when authorization, retention behavior, and disclosures match. Prefer incomplete-but-honest over polished-but-misleading.
9. **Accessibility and calm** — High-consequence work requires legible, keyboard-accessible, reduced-motion-friendly interfaces.
10. **No false security theater** — Do not claim encryption, anonymity, verification, or compliance that the system does not implement.
